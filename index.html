          How to play
          • Press a direction as the gold ring closes on your block. Arrows, WASD, a game controller or the on-screen pad.
          • Perfect hits grow your streak. Every streak tier adds music layers and effects.
          • Bumping walls, pillars and glowing rails is safe. Open edges drop you.
          • Reach the glowing door to clear a room.
          • Levels let you skip beats to think. Endless and Daily need a move every beat.
          • New tiles unlock world by world. The Tile guide on the main menu explains each one.
          • Keyboard: Esc pause or back, R retry, N next level, M mute music, arrows move through menus.
          • No sound on iPhone? Check the ringer switch and volume.

          Tile guide

          The story so far

          • Door. Reach it to clear the room.
          • Rail. A glowing lip on an edge. Bumping it is safe.
          • Open edge. No rail means you fall.
          • Pillar. Blocks you. Bump it to wait a beat.
          • ◆Gem. 250 bonus points times your multiplier.
          • Cracked tile. Falls 2 beats after you land on it.
          • Teleporter. Step in, come out at its twin.
          • ›Arrow lock. You can only leave the way it points.
          • Rhythm gate. Open every other beat. Coral bars mean closed.
          • Button. Step on it to drop every coral gate pillar.
          • Gate pillar. Blocks the path until the button is pressed.
          • »Wind. Pushes you one extra tile the way it flows.
          • Fire vent. Glows, then bursts between beats. Step on it right after it fires.
          • Ice. You slide until you hit solid ground or a wall.
          • Spring pad. Launches you 2 extra tiles the way you were moving, over gaps.
          • Twin gate. A blue pillar that drops only after you press BOTH blue buttons, 1 and 2.
          • Blink tile. Switches on and off every bar. It flickers on beat 4 before it vanishes.
          • Laser row. Charges over each bar and fires just after beat 4. Be off the red line.
          • Ghost tile. Invisible until you stand next to it. Flashes on each downbeat.
          • ◷Duel power-ups. Tempo Drag, Freeze, Scramble, Shield, Mega Shell and Rapid Fire orbs appear on your side. Walk into one to use it.
          • Blaster pad. Bosses and Duels only. Step on it and a shot lands exactly one beat later. A new pad appears elsewhere.
          • Red target. A boss shot lands here 2 beats after it appears. Move off it.
