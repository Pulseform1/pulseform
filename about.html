About Pulseform

Pulseform is a rhythm game played in the browser. You move one tile at a time, and every move has to land on the beat of the music. Each level has its own song, and the music builds as you play in time.

What players practice

  • Rhythm: keeping a steady pulse and feeling where each beat falls.
  • Timing: landing moves on the beat. The game shows whether each one was early, late or on time, and a calibration tool adjusts for headphone and speaker delay.
  • Listening: following the song closely, because every level is built around its own track and tempo.
  • Musical patterns: spotting repeating beat patterns and tempo changes, and planning moves around them.
  • Practice: short levels with stars to earn, so getting better means replaying and refining, the same way you'd practice an instrument.

How it works

Levels are grouped into worlds that slowly raise the tempo and add new rules. There's an optional on-screen metronome, a Daily level, and a level editor for making your own. Progress is saved on the device, or in the cloud if you sign in.

Every level in Pulseform can be played for free.
