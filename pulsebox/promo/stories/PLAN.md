# STUCK IN PULSEBOX: a 3-episode story series (plan, for approval)

Milo built Pulsebox. His best friend is Jay. Each episode, a Pulsebox game pulls them both inside. They escape
using Milo's knowledge of how the game works, the episode ends well, and the last shot teases the next game.
Every rule, monster and gadget in the plots below is a real mechanic of that Pulsebox game, taken from its
description in `public/index.html`.

- **Format:** 9:16, about 65 to 75 s each, the same hand-drawn 2D look. Title card: "STUCK IN PULSEBOX · EP 1".
- **Voices (all Kokoro, free):** the narrator is the Sky + Emma blend (the series voice); Milo is `am_puck`; Jay is
  `am_echo`. Captions are colour-coded by speaker (Milo lime, Jay orange, narrator white).
- **Characters:** Milo is the purple hoodie with lime headphones, the developer from video 1. Jay is the orange
  hoodie with blond hair, from the friends video.
- **Pulsebox:** the game loads with a Pulsebox loading screen when they're pulled in (box.pulseform.org in the
  address bar). After they escape, a short "this game is real" reveal shows its Pulsebox card, then a tease
  for the next episode.

### Why people keep watching

- Each hook is a concrete danger in the first frame (hands out of a screen, a shark fin, a mansion heist), and the
  question "how do they get out?" isn't answered until the end.
- A small win, a new threat, then a bigger threat, about every 5 to 8 seconds.
- Milo always wins by knowing his own game, which means a viewer who plays it knows the same tricks.
- A good ending every time (they get home and laugh, keep a souvenir), plus a tease of the next game, so the series binges.
- EP 3 asks the comments to pick the next game, which is free content ideas and engagement.
