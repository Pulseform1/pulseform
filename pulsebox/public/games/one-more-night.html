TEN NIGHTS. THREE SURVIVORS. ONE WAY HOME.

Keep the lights on.

  1. DAYLIGHT IS BORROWED TIME.

    Explore the forest for wood, repair parts, food, batteries, and clues. Bring supplies home before nightfall.

  2. THE CABIN IS YOUR LAST LINE.

    Stand by a window, door, generator, or radio and open BUILD. Supplies come from your own pack. Keep the generator fuelled.

  3. STAY TOGETHER. STAY QUIET.

    Running and light can give you away. Hold USE beside a downed teammate to revive them. Your axe uses stamina.

  4. FOLLOW THE SIGNAL.

    Read your journal and explore marked places. Repair the radio and find its three-digit code. Retrieve the bridge’s tuning crystal and calibrate the receiver from night eight. Restore the tower from night nine, then survive ten nights to prepare an escape.
