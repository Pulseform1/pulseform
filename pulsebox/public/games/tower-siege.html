How to play

Build towersTap an empty grass spot next to the road, pick a tower, then tap it again to build.
Stop the enemiesEnemies walk down the road toward your castle. Each one that gets in costs you a heart.
UpgradeTap a tower to upgrade or sell it. At level 3 you pick one of two elite towers.
Your heroTap your hero (or the portrait) then tap the map to move. Use the ability button for a big attack.
SpellsTap a spell, then tap the map to cast it. They recharge over time.
Call waves earlyTap the skull to send the next wave sooner and earn bonus gold.

Keys: 1 2 3 spells · Q hero ability · H select hero · Space next wave · F speed · P pause
