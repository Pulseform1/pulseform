How to play

SteerLeft and right arrows (or A and D). On a phone, hold the left or right side of the screen.
NitroUp arrow or Space. On a phone, hold the NITRO button. Near misses and wrecks refill it.
Lose the copsMake them crash into walls, trees and each other to wreck them for big points.
Don't stop!If you slow down next to a cop, you get BUSTED. If your health runs out, you're WRECKED.
StarsThe longer you last, the more stars. More stars means SWAT trucks, roadblocks, helicopters and tanks.
Power-upsShield, magnet, EMP, repair, nitro and double coins. Grab them on the road.
