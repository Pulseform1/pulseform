How to Play

    
Drive to the 🏁 finish without crashing your helmet or falling off. Faster times earn more stars. Grab bolts to buy new paint, helmets and wheels.

    

      
⌨ Keyboard
    
        
  • W or ↑ gas
  • S or ↓ brake, hold to reverse
    • 
        
  • A D or ← → tilt in the air, lean on the ground
    • 
        
  • R restart   C back to checkpoint
  • Esc or P pause   M mute

      
📱 Touch
    
        
  • Hold GAS (right) to drive
  • Hold BRAKE to slow down or back up
    • 
        
  • TILT buttons (left) spin you in the air
  • Tap ⟲ to go back to the last checkpoint

    

    
Tips: land with all wheels at once. Flips in the air give bonus bolts. Walls and crates break if you hit them fast enough. Wait on platforms until they stop.
