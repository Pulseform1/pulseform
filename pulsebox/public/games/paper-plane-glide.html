How to play

1. Tap once to pick your angle, then tap again when the power is in the green for a perfect throw.
2. Hold anywhere (or Space) to tip your nose up. Let go to glide down and pick up speed.
3. Don't climb too slowly or you'll stall. Dive a little, then pull up!
4. Ride heater vents and fans. Bounce on trampolines. Fly through hoops.
5. Tap FLAP (F key) for a quick lift and hold ROCKET (R key) once you've bought them.
6. Spend your coins in the Hangar and throw again to fly farther!
