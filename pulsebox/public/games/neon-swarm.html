How to play

1Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere. That's it: your weapons fire on their own.
2Grab the crystals enemies drop. Fill the bar at the top to level up and pick a new weapon or upgrade.
3Survive until the timer runs out, then beat the boss to clear the level.
4Evolve! Max out a weapon, own its matching upgrade, then open a treasure chest from an elite.
5Spend chips in the Workshop on upgrades, robots, weapons and paint jobs.
