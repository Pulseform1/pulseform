    THE SHOPPING TRIP OF YOUR LIFE

    Shop smart. Move fast.

    01

    GET YOUR LIST.

    Pick a difficulty. A random list chooses real merchandise from across SUNDOT. The map shows departments, not item locations.

    02

    FILL YOUR CART.

    Find a listed item. It highlights when you’re close and can see it. Press E to collect. Use the moving ramps to reach the other floor.

    03

    LOSE SECURITY.

    Guards patrol and chase when they see you. Sprint to gain distance, turn corners, and stay out of sight. Rest to recover stamina. On Easy and Medium, getting caught first earns a warning and +10 seconds.

    04

    CHECK OUT.

    After the last item, return to the downstairs registers and press E. The stopwatch stops there. Beat your own time on the next list.
