How to play

Move and jumpArrow keys or WASD. Up or Space jumps. On a phone, use the stick on the left.
Punch and kickJ punches, K kicks (or Z and X). Mix them for combos: Punch Punch Punch, Punch Punch Kick, Kick Kick...
Block and rollHold Down or Shift to block. Tap left or right while blocking to roll away.
SpecialL or C uses energy (the blue bar). Hold Up, Down or Forward for other specials once you learn them.
Grab and throwI or V grabs. Then punch to knee them or press grab again to throw. With a weapon, grab throws the weapon!
WeaponsWalk over a weapon to pick it up. Crates and the sky drop new ones. They break after a while.
Finish them!When a foe is almost out of health, get close and press Special for your finishing move.
HazardsWatch out for falling crates, spikes, zappy floors and lava. Knock your foes into them!
