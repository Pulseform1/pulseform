How to play

Planet Gravelle is getting cold. Deep below, at the very core, its glowing heart has gone to sleep. Hop in your drill pod, dig down through ten wild layers and wake it up!

DigLeft and right arrows (or A and D) drill sideways. Down arrow (or S) drills down. On a phone, use the arrow pad.
FlyHold Up (or W) to fire your thrusters. You cannot drill up, so fly up your tunnels to get home.
Fuel and cargoDigging and flying burn fuel. Your cargo hold fills with ore. Fly back to the base pad before you run out!
Sell and upgradeLand on the base pad to sell ore. Buy a better drill for harder rock and a better hull to go deeper.
DangersGas pockets, falling boulders, lava, cave worms and crushing pressure. Your drill can fight worms!
ItemsKeys 1 to 4: Dynamite, Fuel can, Repair kit and Teleporter. Buy them at the base.
