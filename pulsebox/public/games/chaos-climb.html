How to play

GoalHelp Nib reach the green EXIT door in every room. Rooms get harder as you climb up through 4 parts of the lab.
KeyboardA D or ← → move.
Space, W or ↑ jump. Hold to jump higher.
R go back to the last flag. Esc or P pause.
TouchUse the arrow buttons to move and the pink button to jump. The round button sends you back to the last flag. Pause is at the top.
Every trap warns youLook for cracks, little holes, dotted wires, flickering floors, shaking blocks and red eyes. Died? Look again. The clue was there!
Flags and chipsTouch a flag to save your spot. Grab the green data chip in each room. 4 chips in a part of the lab opens its secret vault.
Beat the targetEach room has a target time. Beat it to earn a star. Your best times and fewest deaths are saved on this device.
