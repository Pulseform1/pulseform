How to play

SteerUse the arrow keys or A and D. On a phone, hold the left or right side of the screen.
Stay onThe ball rolls by itself and keeps getting faster. Don't roll off the edge or into a hole.
No red!Red blocks and spinning bars end your run. Yellow ramps launch you over gaps.
Close callsSlip past red things by a hair to get CLOSE! and raise your combo. The combo multiplies your score.
GemsGrab pink gems. Spend them in the shop on new balls, trails and worlds, or on a revive.
ZonesEvery 500 m the colors shift. Every 1000 m you enter a new zone and get bonus gems.
