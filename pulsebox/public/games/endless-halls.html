Difficulty

Difficulty changes how fast monsters move and how long batteries last. It starts on the next floor.

HOW TO SURVIVE

Find 3 fusesEach floor hides three glowing fuses. Bring them to the EXIT door to power it and get out.
Follow the beepA beep gets faster as you get closer to the next fuse. With headphones, it also tells you which side it is on.
Your flashlightF turns it on and off. Batteries run out, so pick up spare ones.
Your mindDarkness and monsters scare you. When your mind gets low, you start seeing things. Clean water helps.
RunHold Shift to run, but running is loud and makes you run out of breath.
Every monster is differentRead the notes you find. They tell you how to survive what is on each floor.
