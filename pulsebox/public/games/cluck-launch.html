CLUCKLAUNCH!

Slide, fly and blast one brave chicken all the way to space!

How to play

1. Slide!Press Space or tap to slide down the snowy ramp and jump off the end.
2. TiltArrow keys (or hold the left / right side of the screen) tip your nose up or down. A little nose up = long glide.
3. RocketHold Space or the ROCKET button to blast off while you have fuel.
4. UpgradeEvery flight earns coins. Buy better gear, fly higher, and reach space!
Grab coins, pop balloons for a lift, and bounce on pink clouds. Watch out for birds, planes and UFOs up high!
