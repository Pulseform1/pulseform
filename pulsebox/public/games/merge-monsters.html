How to play

1. Hatch eggsTap Buy Egg to get a baby monster. Free eggs also drop on your board. Tap them to hatch.
2. Merge twinsDrag a monster onto the same monster. They merge and evolve! Each monster has 5 stages. Fully grown ones can get stars.
3. Build a teamDrag your best monsters into the 5 team slots. Slot 1 stands in front and gets hit first.
4. Battle!Fights play by themselves. Win coins, gems and chests. Beat bosses every 10 stages.
Type wheelEach element beats the next one: Fire beats Leaf, Leaf beats Shadow, Shadow beats Spark, Spark beats Water, Water beats Fire.
TipsYour board makes coins every second. Spend them on eggs and on Power Training in the Shop. Drag a monster to SELL if you need room. Keys: arrows + Space to move monsters, E = egg, B = battle.
