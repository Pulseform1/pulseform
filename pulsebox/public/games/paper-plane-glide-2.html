How to play

1. Tap once to pick your angle, then tap again when the power is in the green for a perfect throw.
2. Hold anywhere (or Space) to tip your nose up. Let go to glide down and pick up speed.
3. Don't climb too slowly or you'll stall. Dive a little, then pull up!
4. Ride vents, fans, geysers and lava heat. Bounce on trampolines and clouds. Fly through hoops and boost rings.
5. Chain coins, hoops and rings quickly for a COMBO multiplier. Grab gift boxes and hidden paper animals.
6. Watch out for snow gusts, lightning, lava bombs and meteors far from home.
7. Tap FLAP (F key) for a quick lift and hold ROCKET (R key) once you've bought them.
8. Spin the Prize Wheel, claim daily gifts, level up your Sky Pass, and spend coins in the Hangar. Then throw again!
