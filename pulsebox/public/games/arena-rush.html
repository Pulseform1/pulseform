How to play

Goal

  • Grab energy cells. Each one is 10 points. Gold cells are worth 3.
  • Get hit and you drop cells. Hit rivals for +15, knock them out for +50.
  • Most points when the timer ends wins. The last 15 seconds are Overcharge: cells count double!
  • Drive over ? pods for gadgets. Hold a hard turn at speed to drift, let go for a boost.

Controls

  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows to drive, Space gadget, Shift boost, Esc or P pause.
  • Phone: drag on the left side to drive where you point. Tap GADGET and BOOST on the right.
  • Prefer pointing where you want to go on a keyboard too? Pick Easy steering in Settings.
  • Rivals marked CPU are computer bots, not real people.
