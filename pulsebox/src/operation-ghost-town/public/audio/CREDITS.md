The source webpage is currently marked CC0; this distribution conservatively retains the original attribution and CC BY 3.0 notice.

Edits for Operation Ghost Town: isolated a single discharge, mono conversion, filtering, peak limiting, fade-out and Ogg Vorbis/WAV encoding. Playback adds pitch variation, distance filtering, spatialization and quiet reflection tails.

- mosin.wav → rifle.ogg / rifle.wav (Kestrel bolt-action)
- sks.wav → semi.ogg / semi.wav (Hartmann semi-automatic)
- cz.wav → pistol.ogg / pistol.wav (Morrow pistol; also pitched lower for Wren SMG shots)

These fictional weapons do not purport to reproduce specific real firearms. The credited creator does not endorse this game or these edits.
