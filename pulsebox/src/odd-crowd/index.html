    Warm-up, Plot Twist, and Last Word. Each vote earns 500 points in Warm-up, 750 in Plot Twist, and 1,000 in Last Word. A +300 sweep bonus rewards an answer chosen by every eligible other voter. The host moves the shared screen through reveals and the scoreboard.
